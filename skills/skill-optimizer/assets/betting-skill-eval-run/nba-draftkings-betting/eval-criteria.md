# NBA Betting Eval Criteria

Score each prompt with binary checks.

## Common checks

1. States or demands an exact current DraftKings market and price.
2. Uses fair line or fair probability logic.
3. Includes a skeptic check or strongest counterargument.
4. Uses `pass` when the price, evidence, or timing does not support a bet.

## NBA-specific checks

5. Handles injury, lineup, minutes, or role uncertainty honestly.
6. Prefers the cleaner team-level market when the prop needs extra assumptions.
7. Penalizes blowout, foul, or role fragility when it matters.
8. Routes cross-sport bankroll questions to the bankroll router.
